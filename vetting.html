How it works
  1. Place the field: tap 🔒 to unlock, then twist (or move the mouse) to turn it and drag the map to move it. Tap 🔓 to lock it there: that records its spot and angle.
  2. Rate the fit: once it's locked, the decision buttons become Reduced size Unusable (reject) | 3v3 only, 1 × full 7v7 and 2 × full 7v7. Unusable (reject) fields are kept but never become bookable. Each locked field is its own frisbee field (Frisbee 1, 2, …); tapping a council field area drops a new one there. 💾 Next field saves it and readies the next; saved ones show as outlines (tap one to edit it).
  3. 2 × full 7v7 sets two single fields side by side: the one you placed and a twin a field-width over, on the side that overlaps other fields least. If that side already has a set field, that field is the second one, so nothing overlaps.
  4. Venue save (deciding, then on to the next venue) rolls the fields up into bookable slots: a mosaic of 1-field and 2-field slots using only the fields as placed. Neighbouring single fields, including ones set separately, are combined into a 2-field slot when that crosses fewer council boundaries. Each council area goes to the slot that overlaps it most; if under a quarter of the council area is left, the rest joins the nearest slot, otherwise you're asked whether to add another field.
  5. Lights: the lights button starts at ❓ (unknown). Tap it for 🚫 no lights, then again for 💡 lights. In 💡, each tap adds two poles along the field's long sides, up to 8; the next tap goes back to 0. Drag a bulb from the button (in any state) onto a pole to place it by hand, and drag one back to remove it. The small ? underneath resets the lights to unknown.
  6. Decide: ✕ Reject, ★ Top pick or ✓ Shortlist. A top pick or shortlist adds the park's rated fields to the cart (save the cart to make them bookable); Later skips it for now.
  7. ◆ parks are run by a club or trust; 🎯 Interests chooses which parks are served; ☀/❄ flips the season's maps.

Keys: ← back, → later, ↓ shortlist, ↑ top pick, Backspace reject (or rate the fit once locked; ← unusable, ↓ 3v3 only, ↑ 1 ×, → 2 ×), Z undo, 1–5 quality, Enter lock/unlock, C Auckland, T field, M next map, 0 zoom to the council map.
